## Game Rules
- Press the left and right arrow keys to move your plane
- Press the space bar to fire bullets
- A yellow block drops after defeating every 10 enemies; touching it will upgrade your bullets
- The boss spawns after you defeat 100 enemies, and you win the game by defeating the boss
- The game ends immediately if you collide with enemies, enemy bullets or meteorites
